# Pixel Pour – Privacy Policy **Last updated: 4 November 2026** Pixel Pour is a sand-art puzzle game. We have built it to collect as little as possible: there are **no accounts, no sign-ups and no servers of our own**. Your progress is kept on your device. This policy explains what information the game uses, who else is involved (Apple, Google and Unity), and the choices you have. ## 1. Who we are Pixel Pour is made by **ObiWaz Games** (Warren Davison-Wood, trading as ObiWaz Games, United Kingdom) ("we", "us"), who is the data controller for the game. - Email: **wmdavison7@aol.com** If you have any question about privacy, or want to use any of your rights (section 8), please contact us. ## 2. Information kept on your device (not sent to us) The game saves the following **only on your device**, so it can remember where you are. We never receive it. - Your progress: levels played, stars, best match, badges, gallery pictures, daily picture and streak, quests. - Your Drops balance and the looks you own and use. - Your settings (music, sound, vibration, colour patterns, text size, tips seen). - Your answer to the age question: **only whether you are under 16 or not**, never your birth date or year. - Your privacy choices from the consent screen. - Records of in-app purchases (the product and the store's transaction number), so each purchase is given once and can be restored. - How many optional reward ads you have watched today (there is a daily limit). You can delete all of it at any time with **Settings › Delete my data**, or by deleting the game. ## 3. Information handled by other companies ### Advertising – Google AdMob The game shows **optional** ads: a banner on the main menu, and short videos you can choose to watch for a reward. We use Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited for users in the UK and EEA) to show them. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect information from your device, such as: - device and app information (device model, operating system, app version) and an advertising or app identifier; - your IP address and approximate location derived from it; - how ads are shown and interacted with, and diagnostic information. **Your choice comes first.** When you first open the game, we ask your age and then show Google's consent screen (Google's User Messaging Platform) where the law requires it, including in the UK and EEA. No ad is requested before that step is finished. - If you agree, Google may show **personalised** ads. - If you don't, Google shows **non-personalised** ads, which are based on things like the app and your general location rather than your profile. Google may still use some information for limited purposes such as fraud prevention and measuring ad delivery. - You can change your choice at any time in **Settings › Privacy settings**. - **Players under 16** only ever get child-directed, non-personalised ads, with Google's under-age-of-consent and child-directed settings switched on. - We **don't** ask to track you across other companies' apps (Apple's tracking prompt), so the game never has access to your device's advertising identifier on iPhone. How Google uses information: and Google's privacy policy: . **Remove Ads**: if you buy Remove Ads, the banner stops. Reward videos stay available if you choose to watch them. ### Purchases – Apple App Store and Google Play You can buy Drops, the Deluxe Sand Pack and Remove Ads. Payment is handled entirely by **Apple** (App Store) or **Google** (Google Play) under their own terms and privacy policies. We never see your payment card or account details; the game only receives which product was bought and the store's transaction number. Nothing in the game is random and nothing is needed to play. ### Leaderboards and friends – Game Center and Google Play Games Leaderboards and friends are **optional**. If you choose to join (from the Leaderboards or Friends screens), your score totals are sent to **Apple Game Center** (iPhone) or **Google Play Games** (Android), shown under your Game Center or Play Games name. Friends lists, friend requests and any contact matching are handled by Apple or Google with their own permissions; **the game never reads your contacts**. Apple's privacy policy: . ### Sharing and challenges When you share a picture, a time-lapse, an invite or a challenge, the game opens your phone's share sheet and you choose where it goes. A challenge code only contains the level, the score, the match and the time; it contains no name or personal information. We don't receive anything you share. ### The game engine – Unity Pixel Pour is made with Unity. The Unity engine sends limited, anonymous technical information about the device (such as the device model, operating system, graphics hardware and screen) to **Unity Technologies** to produce performance and hardware statistics. Unity's privacy policy: . ### Analytics **The game doesn't currently use any analytics service.** If we add one in future, we will only use it with your consent, never for players under 16, and we will update this policy first. ## 4. Children Pixel Pour is suitable for all ages. We ask a neutral age question when the game first opens and **keep only whether the player is under 16**. For players under 16: - ads are always non-personalised and child-directed; - no analytics is used; - there is no chat and no way to send messages or your own text to other players. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. Purchases are made through Apple or Google, whose parental controls (such as Ask to Buy and Family Link) apply. If you are a parent and have a question, please contact us. ## 6. How long information is kept - **On your device:** until you use **Delete my data** or delete the game. Only the most recent 200 purchase transaction numbers are kept. - **By Google, Apple and Unity:** according to their own policies (linked above). We don't keep any information about you on servers of our own, because we don't have any. ## 7. International transfers Google, Apple and Unity may process information outside the UK and EEA, including in the United States. They use legal safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and the UK's International Data Transfer Addendum. ## 8. Your rights Under the UK GDPR and the EU GDPR you have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to object to or restrict how it is used, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. - **Withdraw or change ad consent:** Settings › Privacy settings. - **Delete everything the game keeps:** Settings › Delete my data. This also clears your consent choices, so the age question and consent screen appear again. - **Information held by Google, Apple or Unity:** contact them directly using the links above; they control that information. - **Anything else:** email us at **wmdavison7@aol.com**. We will reply within one month. If you're unhappy with how your information is handled, you can complain to the **Information Commissioner's Office** (UK): , or to the data-protection authority in your EU country. ## 9. Security Your game data stays on your device and is protected by your device's own security. Purchases and accounts are handled by Apple and Google. The game doesn't contain any passwords or secret keys. ## 10. Changes to this policy If we change how the game uses information, we will update this page and the date at the top before releasing that version. For important changes, we will also tell you in the game.
